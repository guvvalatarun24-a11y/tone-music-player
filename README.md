# Tone

Tone is a modern, offline-first local music player built with React, TypeScript, and Vite. It is designed to play your legally owned music directly in your browser without ever transmitting your audio files to an external server.

Your Music. Your Way.

## Key Features

- **Offline-First Storage**: Music is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. Audio files never leave your device.
- **Smart Shuffle**: True Fisher-Yates shuffle algorithm with intelligent queue management and history tracking to avoid repetitive playback.
- **Repeat Modes**: Repeat OFF, Repeat ALL, and Repeat ONE modes.
- **Responsive High-Resolution Waveform Editor**: Clean unwanted sections (intros, outros, middle cuts) with precision canvas waveform rendering and drag-to-trim controls.
- **Keep Original Files Control**: Option to preserve original files or replace them when saving cleaned versions.
- **ID3 Metadata & Artwork**: Automatic extraction of artist, title, album, and embedded APIC cover art frames.
- **Media Session Integration**: Full support for hardware media keys and system audio controls (play, pause, next, previous, seek).
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable on desktop and mobile with standalone offline caching.
